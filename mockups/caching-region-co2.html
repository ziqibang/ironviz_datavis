Caching cuts the energy; the grid decides how much carbon that is worth

Carbon saved by caching = energy saved by caching × how dirty the local grid is. We combine our energy estimate with the 40 Google Cloud region factors from the CodeCarbon repository. Three ways to draw it are below; I recommend B.

CO₂e saved (g) = kWh saved by caching × grid intensity (gCO₂e / kWh)
campus, 30 days: caching today saves kWh · +10 points of reuse would save kWh more
Before choosing, three caveats: (1) CodeCarbon's table now lists Google Cloud only; AWS and Azure rows were removed in May 2023 (commit 1a987a4). (2) Our central factor, 350 g/kWh, is Azure/OpenAI from Jegham et al., not CodeCarbon. (3) The logs don't say where requests ran, and CMU can't pick the region for most API calls, so every region here is a what-if.
